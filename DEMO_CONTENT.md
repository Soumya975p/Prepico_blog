## Blog 4: Mastering Behavioral Interview Questions

**Title:** Mastering Behavioral Interview Questions: The STAR Method

**Thumbnail URL:** `https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1560250097-0b93528c311a?w=800`

**Content:**

**+ Paragraph:**
Behavioral interview questions are designed to understand how you've handled situations in the past. The STAR method is your secret weapon for answering them effectively.

**+ Heading:**
Understanding the STAR Method

**+ Bullet List:**
- Situation: Set the context for your story
- Task: Describe your responsibility
- Action: Explain what you did
- Result: Share the outcome and what you learned

**+ Paragraph:**
Practice applying this framework to common behavioral questions like "Tell me about a time you faced a challenge" or "Describe a situation where you worked in a team."

**+ Horizontal Layout:**
  - **Paragraph:** Prepare 5-7 strong STAR stories that highlight different skills and competencies.

## Quick Tips for Creating These Blogs:

1. **Go to Admin Page:** http://localhost:5173/admin
2. **Copy content** for each blog section by section
3. **Use the appropriate button** for each content type:
   - Click "+ Paragraph" for paragraph text
   - Click "+ Heading" for headings
   - Click "+ Bullet List" for lists (add items one by one)
   - Click "+ Image" and paste the URL
   - Click "+ Horizontal Layout" for side-by-side content
4. **Save** each blog before moving to the next
